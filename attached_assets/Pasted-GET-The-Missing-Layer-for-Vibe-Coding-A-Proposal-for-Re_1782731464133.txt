The Solution

get

get gives every Replit app a simple editing experience.

The vibe coder builds the app once.

The client safely edits their own content forever.

No code.

No prompts.

No redeployments.

Clients can edit only what the builder has approved.

Everything else stays protected.

Think of it as giving every Replit app a built in content layer without turning it into a CMS.
