# Why I would NOT choose HEAVY right now

The “heavy” option sounds modern, but for your product it’s actually the riskiest move right now.

It would mean:

- deleting your Express API
- moving logic from server routes into frontend + RLS
- replacing your auth model
- replacing your current permission flow
- rethinking audit logging
- rethinking publish flow
- rethinking project-scoped authorization
- depending heavily on perfect RLS design

That is not a security hardening step.  
That is a **security-sensitive rewrite**.

And rewrites are where people accidentally:
- expose data
- weaken checks
- miss edge cases
- lose auditability
- create auth bugs

So if your goal is **“most secure practical environment”**, HEAVY is the wrong move right now.

# Why LIGHT is the secure choice

With LIGHT, you keep your strongest current pattern:

## Server-controlled security
Your backend stays in charge of:
- who the user is
- what project they can access
- what role they have
- whether they can publish
- whether they can invite users
- what domains are allowed
- what gets logged

That’s exactly the architecture I’d want for your product.

For **get**, that matters a lot, because this product has:

Those are better enforced in a server you control than spread across frontend + database policy rewrites.

# Plain-English breakdown of the 3 choices

## LIGHT
**Supabase = database host only**

You keep:
- Express
- Drizzle
- auth
- cookies
- RBAC
- audit logic

You change:
- database connection target

### Best for:
- security now
- least breakage
- fastest move
- reversible architecture

## MEDIUM
**Keep Express, but replace auth with Supabase Auth**

You keep:
- Express routes
- most backend patterns

You change:
- login/session/auth stack

### Best for:
- later, if you decide managed auth is worth it
- reducing custom auth burden
- after the app is stable

### Risk:
You still disrupt a sensitive area: auth.

## HEAVY
**Frontend talks to Supabase directly, RLS becomes the main security layer**

You keep:
- much less of your current backend

You change:
- almost everything important

### Best for:
- greenfield rebuilds
- teams very comfortable with RLS-first architecture
- apps designed around Supabase from day one

### Not best for:
- your current app
- your current timeline
- security-first stabilization
